📜 Riding rules

⚠️ On every ride

  1. Wear your helmet, use lights at dawn and dusk, and carry a working bell.
  2. Ride single file on busy roads. Never overtake the ride leader.
  3. Call out potholes, gravel, dogs, and vehicles approaching from behind.
  4. Regroup at every turn and junction. No one gets left behind.
  5. Carry a spare tube, a pump, your ID, water, and some food.

🤝 Etiquette

  • Be on time at the meeting point. Let the group know if you are running late.
  • Respect local people, villages, and temples along the route.
  • Pack out your rubbish. Keep the coast clean. 🌿
  • If you drop out, tell the ride leader before you head home.

🛂 Rider passport

Stamps are earned on coast legs, sunrise rides, and club rides with 5 or more riders. The ride leader records your stamp.
